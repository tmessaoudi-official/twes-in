## Parties and roles

The company using the service is the controller of the data it records in it. {{publisher.name}} processes that data on the company's behalf, as a processor, under these terms.

## Subject and duration

The processing consists of hosting, storing and processing that data to provide the service to the company: invoicing, customers and vendors, stock, expenses and whatever its modules add. It lasts as long as the company's subscription.

## Data and data subjects

- The company's customers and vendors, and their contacts: names, addresses, e-mail addresses, telephone numbers, tax identifiers.
- The company's members: their account and what they do in the service.
- The people named in the company's documents: invoices, delivery notes, expenses and attachments.

## The processor's commitments

- To process the data only on the company's documented instructions.
- To ensure that the people authorised to process it are bound by confidentiality.
- To implement the security measures described below.
- To use only the sub-processors listed below, and to inform the company of any change.
- To help the company answer requests from people exercising their rights.
- To notify the company of any personal data breach without undue delay after becoming aware of it.
- At the end of the subscription, to return or erase the data as the company chooses, unless the law requires it to be kept.
- To make available to the company the information needed to demonstrate compliance with these commitments, and to allow audits.

## Security measures

- Each company's data is kept apart by the service; one company never reads another's.
- Access to data within a company depends on each member's role and rights.
- Passwords are kept only hashed; a second factor is offered and the company can require it.
- The session cookie is sent only over encrypted connections.
- Actions on the data are written to the company's log: who, what and when.
