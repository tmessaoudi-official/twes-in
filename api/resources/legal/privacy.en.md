## Who is responsible

{{publisher.name}} is responsible for your account's data. The data a company records in the service about its customers, its vendors and its own members is that company's responsibility; the publisher processes it on the company's behalf, under the terms of the "Data processing (DPA)" page.

## What is collected

- Your e-mail address, your display name and the language you choose.
- Your password, kept only in a hashed form from which it cannot be recovered.
- Your second factors: an authenticator app's secret, your passkeys and your recovery codes.
- The date you last signed in, and the number of failed sign-in attempts, which can lock the account for a while.
- The companies you are a member of and your role in each.
- What you do in the service, written to the company's log: who, what and when.
- The notifications addressed to you.

## Why, and on what basis

- To provide the service you use: performance of the contract.
- To protect accounts and data, and to find out who did what: legitimate interest.
- To keep invoices and accounting records: legal obligation.

## Who can see it

- The members of your company, each according to the rights their role gives them.
- The publisher's sub-processors, named on the "Data processing (DPA)" page: the host {{host.name}} and the e-mail sending service {{mail.provider}}.

## How long

- Account data, for as long as the account exists; after it is closed: to be completed.
- Invoices and accounting records, ten years.
- The activity log: to be completed.

## Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, its rectification, its erasure, the restriction of its processing or its portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to {{privacy.email}}. For data a company has recorded about you, contact that company first.

## Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country: in France the CNIL, in Tunisia the Instance nationale de protection des données personnelles (INPDP).
