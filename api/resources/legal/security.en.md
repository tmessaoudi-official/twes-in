## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you have found a security flaw in this service, write to {{security.email}}. This address is also given in the service's /.well-known/security.txt file.

## What is useful to include

- The address or screen concerned.
- The steps that reproduce the problem.
- What you were able to observe or obtain.

## What we ask of you

- Do not access or change other people's data beyond what is needed to demonstrate the problem.
- Do not disrupt the service or damage its data.
- Allow time for a fix before making the problem public.

## What the publisher undertakes

To be completed: the time within which the publisher acknowledges a report and fixes it, and its commitment to people who report in good faith.

## Measures in place

- Each company's data is kept apart by the service.
- Passwords are kept only hashed, and a second factor is offered.
- The session cookie is sent only over encrypted connections.
- Actions on the data are written to the company's log.
